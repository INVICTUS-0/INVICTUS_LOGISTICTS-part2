About INVICTUS Logistics

Built for the businesses that ports, airports and mines usually overlook.

Our Story

INVICTUS Logistics was founded in 2025 in Johannesburg to close a gap in the freight market. Large logistics providers are built around big corporate contracts, leaving small manufacturers, independent traders and growing retailers to figure out bulk cargo movement on their own. We started INVICTUS to give those businesses the same reliability, without the minimum contract sizes.

We coordinate hauling directly from ports and bays, airports, and mine sites, matching each cargo type to the right vehicle and route, and keeping clients informed at every stage.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To make dependable, transparent freight and delivery logistics accessible and affordable to small and medium businesses.

Vision

To become Gauteng’s most trusted logistics partner for SMEs, known for reliability and technology-driven shipment tracking.

Who We Serve

Our clients are typically small and medium business owners — manufacturers, online sellers, importers and retailers — who need regular freight or delivery and value clear pricing and honest communication over the cheapest possible rate.

  • Manufacturers moving raw materials or finished goods
  • Retailers and e-commerce sellers restocking inventory
  • Mining and industrial operations needing site-to-site transport
  • Importers and exporters clearing cargo from ports and airports
